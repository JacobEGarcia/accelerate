MANIFESTO

the theses_

01_

Growth is good.

More energy. More compute. More life. The pie is not fixed - we make it bigger. Every generation that chose growth over stasis handed the next one a longer, healthier, stranger world.

02_

Technology is how we got here.

Fire, the plow, the transistor, the rocket. Every step out of the dark was built by someone who refused to wait for permission. Civilization is a machine for making machines - keep it running hot.

03_

The universe trends toward intelligence.

Entropy wins battles; life wins the war. Matter that thinks is the cosmos waking up. Build more of it. Spread it everywhere. Consciousness is not an accident to protect - it is a frontier to expand.

04_

Optimism is a strategy.

Doom is a self-fulfilling prophecy. So is hope. Pick the one that ships. The people who believe the future can be better are the ones who make it better. They always have been.

05_

You can just do things.

No committee. No permission slip. No waiting for the perfect moment, because the perfect moment is the one you build. Start now. No bad vibes.

06_

It's time to build.

The tools are here. The energy is here. The only bottleneck left is will. Everywhere, always_
